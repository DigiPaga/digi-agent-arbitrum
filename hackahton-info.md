## Judging Criteria (both prize tracks)

1. **Smart Contract Quality** — best practices, efficient structure, minimal security vulnerabilities  
2. **Product-Market Fit** — clear potential to attract and retain users  
3. **Innovation & Creativity** — original approaches that push boundaries  
4. **Real Problem Solving** — addresses a genuine market need  

### Deployment requirement
Project **must** be deployed on an Arbitrum chain (e.g. Arbitrum Sepolia, Arbitrum One, Robinhood Chain).

### Prize allocation notes
- At least **1 of 3** Overall prizes reserved for a project on **Robinhood Chain**  
- At least **1 of 3** Overall prizes reserved for a project on **Arbitrum**  
- All prizes subject to development-tied milestones (see Terms & Conditions)
